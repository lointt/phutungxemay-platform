# Chinh sach van chuyen

Pham vi giao hang: Cua hang giao hang toan quoc thong qua doi tac van chuyen (Giao Hang Nhanh, Giao Hang Tiet Kiem, Viettel Post).

Thoi gian giao hang: Noi thanh TP.HCM va Ha Noi: 1-2 ngay lam viec. Cac tinh thanh khac: 3-5 ngay lam viec. Vung sau vung xa: co the len den 7 ngay lam viec.

Phi van chuyen: Mien phi van chuyen cho don hang tu 300.000 VND tro len. Don hang duoi 300.000 VND phu thu 20.000-30.000 VND tuy khu vuc.

Kiem tra hang truoc khi nhan: Khach hang duoc quyen kiem tra ben ngoai kien hang truoc khi thanh toan (doi voi hinh thuc COD). Neu phat hien hang bi mop/vo, tu choi nhan hang va lien he hotline CSKH ngay.
